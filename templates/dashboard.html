Reports

Use Export CSV above to download an assessment with its data source, timestamp, findings and recommendations. Live exports run a fresh assessment. Empty inventories produce a header-only report.

Assessment rules

  • Read-only operation; all remediation is manual.
  • Idle VM candidate: average CPU below 1% and peak below 10%.
  • Underutilized VM candidate: average CPU below 10% and peak below 50%.
  • VM rules require at least 135 hourly samples over seven days.
  • Unattached disks and unassociated public IPs require ownership and dependency review.
  • Live billing is not integrated. Demo costs are illustrative USD inputs, not Azure price quotes.
  • Demo savings assume 100% recovery for removal candidates and 50% for downsizing candidates; these are not guaranteed.
  • Prototype CPU assessment is limited to the first 20 VMs per refresh.
